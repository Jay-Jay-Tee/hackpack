# Slides and demo

Judges usually get a few minutes with each project, so how you show it counts nearly as much as what you built.

This section covers the parts you show: the slides, the live demo, the video, standing up to present, and how sponsor prizes work. For the order of a pitch and what judges look for, see [Demo and pitch](../guide/06-demo-and-pitch.md) in the guide.

1. [Slides](slides.md): how many, what goes on each one, making them readable, and getting them onto the venue's screen
2. [The live demo](live-demo.md): picking the path, demo data, setting up your laptop, and what to do when it breaks
3. [The demo video](video.md): planning, recording and uploading one on Windows, macOS or Linux
4. [Presenting](presenting.md): who talks, timing, handovers, questions, and table-style judging
5. [Sponsors](sponsors.md): sponsor prizes, credits, mentors, and making their part easy to spot

Start the slides and the demo script around the halfway point of the event. The [pitch outline](../templates/PITCH.md) and [submission checklist](../templates/CHECKLIST.md) go with these pages.
